Como Funciona

Capellaris works as a distributed brain for your organization: specialised AI agents working together on tasks too complex for one of them alone.

flowchart LR A[👤 User] -->|Sends a request| B[🧠 Capellaris] B -->|Identifies the need| C{Routing} C -->|Simple task| D[🤖 Single agent] C -->|Complex task| E[👥 Agent squad] D -->|Response| F[📋 Result] E -->|Collaboration| F F -->|Delivery| A

The process in four steps

1

You make a request

In Slack, Teams or the web interface, you describe what you need in plain language.

2

The system reads and routes it

Capellaris works out what you need and picks the right agent or squad for it.

3

Agents work the task

The specialised agents process the request, collaborating where that helps.

4

You get the result

The answer comes back formatted and ready to use, in the channel you chose.

Agent squads

A squad is a temporary team of agents brought together for a task that needs more than one speciality.

When is a squad formed?

  • When the task spans several areas of knowledge
  • When a project needs a complete analysis
  • Quando diferentes perspectivas precisam ser consideradas
  • When you explicitly ask for a multidisciplinary team

Example: a product launch squad

flowchart TB subgraph Squad["👥 Launch squad"] direction TB L[🎯 Lead: Marketing] M[📊 Marketing] F[💰 Finance] V[🤝 Sales] O[⚙️ Operations] end T[📋 Task: plan the launch] --> L L --> M L --> F L --> V L --> O M --> R[📑 Consolidated report] F --> R V --> R O --> R R --> E[✅ Entrega Final] style L fill:#fef3c7 style E fill:#dcfce7

The squad lead

  • Coordinates the work of the other agents
  • Distributes the work sensibly
  • Consolidates the results into one answer
  • Owns the quality of what is delivered

The squad members

  • Executam suas tarefas especializadas
  • Share what is relevant
  • Contribute their own expertise
  • Work towards the shared result

Workflows

Capellaris offers several ways to organise the agents' work, matched to the nature of the task.

Sequencial

Agents work in sequence, each starting after the previous one finishes.

flowchart LR A[Agent 1] --> B[Agent 2] --> C[Agent 3] --> D[Result] style D fill:#dcfce7

Best for: processes where each step depends on the one before.

Paralelo

Agents work at the same time on independent tasks, which saves time.

flowchart TB A[Task] --> B[Agent 1] A --> C[Agent 2] A --> D[Agent 3] B --> E[Result] C --> E D --> E style E fill:#dcfce7

Best for: independent analyses that need consolidating.

Colaborativo

Agents work together, sharing information in real time.

flowchart TB A[Agent 1] <--> B[Agent 2] B <--> C[Agent 3] A <--> C A --> D[Resultado] B --> D C --> D style D fill:#dcfce7

Best for: complex problems that need several perspectives.

Hierarchical

A lead agent coordinates the work, delegating and consolidating the results.

flowchart TB A[🎯 Lead] --> B[Agent 1] A --> C[Agent 2] A --> D[Agent 3] B --> A C --> A D --> A A --> E[Result] style E fill:#dcfce7

Best for: tasks that need central coordination.
